Common fresher hiring flow

  1. Application or campus drive: resume shortlisting, usually with a minimum CGPA or percentage cut-off (often 60%).
  2. Online assessment: aptitude, logical reasoning, verbal and basic coding (1 to 3 hours).
  3. Technical interview: your projects, DSA, one core language, DBMS, OS, networks.
  4. Managerial or HR round: communication, relocation, bond or notice terms, salary talk.
  5. Offer and onboarding: documents check, background verification, joining training.
